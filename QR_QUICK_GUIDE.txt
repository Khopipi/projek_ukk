## 📱 How It Works

```
Print QR Code on Surat
        ↓
User scans with phone
        ↓
Browser opens URL:
/pengajuan/ttd?p={token}
        ↓
Laravel finds pengajuan
        ↓
Displays verification page
        ↓
Shows Kepala Desa name:
H. Saiful Imaduddin, SKM., M.Kes
        ↓
Shows surat details
        ↓
User sees verification status
```
